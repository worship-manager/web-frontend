The most important habit in Worship Manager is treating **Preview** and **Program** as two different stages. Preview is where you prepare. Program is what the congregation sees when Present is running.

If you have used broadcast or live production tools, this will feel familiar. If not, this post will save you from accidental live mistakes.

## The layout at a glance

At the top of the control window:

- **Preview** sits on the left — thumbnails, video, or capture of what you selected last from the bottom tabs
- **Program** sits on the right — a live miniature of the output, plus transport controls for video when needed

Below that are your selectors: Pinned, Songs, Bible, Media, and History.

## What Preview is for

When you click a song lyric, a Bible verse, or a media file, Worship Manager updates **Preview** first. That lets you:

- Confirm you picked the right file or verse
- Scrub through PDF / PowerPoint slide thumbnails
- Check video or camera capture before it goes out
- Choose a slide index for multi-page media

Nothing in Preview has to be live yet. Use it as your “next up” space.

## What Program is for

**Program** mirrors the actual display output. When Present is on, this is effectively what is on the projector or NDI feed.

From the Program area you can:

- **PRESENT** / **STOP** — open or close the display window
- **CLEAR DISPLAY** — remove background media and overlay text
- **CLEAR OVERLAY** — remove only the text overlay (songs / scripture)
- Step through multi-slide media with the previous / next arrows
- Play, pause, seek, and adjust volume for videos

## A safe operator habit

Use this loop every time:

1. Select content in Songs, Bible, or Media
2. Glance at **Preview** — is this the right thing?
3. Take it to the display / confirm it appears in **Program**
4. Only then look away from the control screen

For songs and Bible, clicking a lyric or verse updates the overlay on the live output. For media, selecting from Preview / Program is how you commit a background or slide. Practice once so you know exactly when something becomes live.

## Clearing without panic

Two clears cover most “oh no” moments:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **CLEAR OVERLAY** | Removes song/scripture text; background media stays |
| **CLEAR DISPLAY** | Clears both media and overlay |

There are also keybinds for clear overlay, clear display, and toggle Present — see the [keyboard shortcuts cheat sheet](/blog/keyboard-shortcuts-cheat-sheet).

## Fade transitions

In Settings → Display, you can enable **Fade transitions when switching content**. When that is on, Worship Manager fades between overlays and media instead of hard-cutting. Useful for worship; turn it off if you need snappy announcement slides.

## History and Pinned as your safety net

- **History** remembers recent songs, verses, and media so you can jump back without hunting again
- **Pinned** is for items you know you will need mid-service — pin from the context menu on songs, verses, media, or capture devices

Together with Preview/Program, those tabs keep the service calm even when the order changes.
