Most Sundays start with songs. Worship Manager keeps your lyric library local, searchable, and click-to-present — so the operator can stay with the band instead of fighting slides.

## Open the Songs tab

Press `Ctrl + S` or click **Songs** in the bottom tabs.

You will see:

- A **Search** field for filtering titles
- **Blank Display** — send lyrics without a background
- **ADD SONG** — create a new entry
- **SYNC SONGS** — share/receive libraries between machines ([full guide](/blog/syncing-songs-between-computers))
- **Settings** — fonts, colors, overlay layout for song text
- The lyric list for the selected song on the right

## Add a song

1. Click **ADD SONG**.
2. Enter a title.
3. Add lyrics as separate verses / sections (each block becomes a clickable slide).
4. Use the verse controls to move a section up/down, insert another below, or delete one.
5. Save.

A good rule of thumb: one slide per musical phrase the congregation needs to sing — not one giant wall of text.

## Edit or delete a song

Right-click the song title for context actions:

- Edit the title and lyric blocks
- Delete the song from the library
- **Pin Song** for quick access in the Pinned tab

After edits, the search list refreshes so your team always sees the latest version on that machine.

## Present lyrics live

1. Search and select the song.
2. Click a lyric block — it goes to the overlay on Program / the display.
3. Click the next block when the band moves on.
4. Use **CLEAR OVERLAY** when the song ends but you want the background to stay, or **CLEAR DISPLAY** for a full wipe.

### Blank Display

Turn on **Blank Display** when you want clean white (or styled) text with no media behind it. Leave it off when you are pairing lyrics with a background from the Media tab.

## Style your song overlays

Open **Settings** in the Songs tab to control:

- Font family and size
- Text color and shadow
- Alignment and vertical position (top / center / bottom)
- Background color and background image
- Background mode (tight / normal / full)

Changes apply to song overlays so you can match your church’s look without rebuilding slides every week.

## Pin songs you always use

Right-click a song → **Pin Song**. Pinned items show up under the **Pinned** tab (`Ctrl + P`) for one-click access during service — great for the doxology, response songs, or anything that might appear out of order.

## Tips for a healthy library

- Use consistent titles (`Amazing Grace` not `amazing grace (final)`)
- Split choruses and bridges into their own blocks so you can jump around
- Keep a short “service set” pinned on Sunday morning
- Sync libraries between the prep laptop and the booth PC before you leave the office
